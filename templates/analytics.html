METRIC GLOSSARY

How this page calculates its insights

Transparent definitions make the analysis easier to explain during project demonstrations.

01
Threat Exposure Rate Malicious Files ÷ Total Scans × 100

Measures how much of the stored portfolio is classified as malicious.

02
Critical Share of Threats Critical Threats ÷ Malicious Files × 100

Shows how concentrated the threat queue is in its highest-priority category.

03
Clean Coverage Safe Files ÷ Total Scans × 100

Shows the share of stored records classified as safe.

04
Evidence Maturity Interpretation based on Total Scans

Communicates whether aggregate percentages are based on a limited, emerging, established or broad sample.
